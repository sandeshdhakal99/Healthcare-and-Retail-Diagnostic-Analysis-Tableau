# Healthcare-and-Retail-Diagnostic-Analysis-Tableau
Tableau-based diagnostic and descriptive analysis of healthcare and retail datasets. Explores patient demographics, medical outcomes, admissions, billing insights, and retail sales trends to identify patterns, performance gaps, and data-driven recommendations for decision-making.

## Project Overview
This Tableau project applies **descriptive and diagnostic analytics** to two real-world datasets:
1. **Patient Healthcare Data**
2. **Retail Sales Data**

The objective is to identify trends, patterns, anomalies, and root causes that support better **operational, financial, and clinical decision-making**.

---

## Healthcare Case Study
### Dataset Includes:
- Patient demographics (gender, blood type)
- Medical conditions
- Admissions over time
- Test results
- Insurance providers
- Billing amounts
- Length of hospital stay

### Key Insights:
- Balanced demographic distribution reduces analytical bias
- Arthritis and Cancer show slightly higher abnormal test results
- Diabetes and Hypertension show relatively balanced outcomes
- Admissions peaked in 2020, stabilized through 2023, and dropped sharply in 2024
- Obesity and Hypertension generate the highest average billing
- Longer hospital stays correlate with higher billing, especially for Arthritis and Cancer

---

## Retail Diagnostic Analysis
### Dataset Includes:
- Sales by year and quarter
- Region-wise performance
- Product categories
- Order quantity and average order value

### Key Insights:
- Sales peaked in 2018, followed by decline
- Q2 shows the sharpest sales drop across regions
- Technology category drives sales but declines after Q1
- Central and South regions contribute most to the decline
- Customers place more orders but with lower average order value
